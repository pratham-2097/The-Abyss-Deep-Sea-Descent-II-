The Abyss: Deep-Sea Descent II

Pratham Prabhu · Computer Graphics I, University of Cincinnati · Project 2: Cube World

In Project 1 I made Deep-Sea Descent, a dive through an underwater scene drawn with lines and triangles. This time the ocean is a full 3D world in WebGL made only out of cubes. The seafloor comes from a height map and a color image, and a submarine (finally) cruises through the water with fish and jellyfish on Bezier curves.
